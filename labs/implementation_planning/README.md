# Implementation Planning

**Duration:** 20 min (12 min individual, 8 min pairs)
**Tools:** `standards_worksheet.md` — write actual YAML, not descriptions

## Your task

Take 2 standards from your Module 1 draft and convert each into a runnable check:

- **Standard 1** → a dbt test (write the schema YAML)
- **Standard 2** → a Soda check (write the SodaCL YAML)

Use `standards_worksheet.md`. Write the actual YAML — even if rough. A sketch is better than a description.

## Pair review

Swap worksheets with a partner and check:
- Is each standard **measurable**? Could someone verify it's being met tomorrow?
- Does each standard have a **named owner** — a person, not just "the team"?
- Is the blocker specific? "I need access" is not a blocker — "I need read access to prod DuckDB from [person]" is.

## Share with the group

- Read out one check you wrote (with the YAML)
- Share your single biggest blocker

## Reflection

What one conversation do you need to have this week to unblock your implementation?
